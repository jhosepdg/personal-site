↳ 01 / About

A MIND FOR
COMPLEX THINKING.
A PURPOSE
ROOTED IN PEOPLE.

I am Pepe, a problem solver by training,
a builder by practice, and a global thinker by experience.

Mathematics taught me to stay with a difficult question. Through complex problem sets, I learned to look for patterns and work towards a solution with rigor.

My doctoral studies deepened my humanistic perspectives to challenge assumptions and embrace human judgement. MEITE increased my interests in the learning sciences and personalization perspectives.

Consulting taught me to turn that deep and complex thinking into execution: optimize processes, reduce the time it takes to deliver, and build systems that work, scale, and impact.

Today, I bring this new thinking together through education, entrepreneurship, and emerging technology. The question behind my work is simple: how can we embrace new ways of AI-human thinking to create more possibilities for people?
